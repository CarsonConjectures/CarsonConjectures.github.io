Mean

The average: add the values and divide by their count. x̄ is one sample's mean; μ is the population mean. The mean of means averages all the sample means collected here.

Standard error (SE)

The standard deviation of the sample means: SE = σ / √n. It measures how much the mean varies from sample to sample. Quadrupling n halves SE.

Confidence interval (CI)

Each sample immediately gets x̄ ± 1.96 × SE, using known σ. This method captures μ in about 95% of repeated samples when the normal model fits; small n with other shapes can give poor coverage. The shaded 95% range describes the spread of sample means, not a CI for μ.
